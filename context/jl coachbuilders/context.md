## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Caravans and motorhomes suffer from specialized damage (water ingress, rot, composite/fiberglass cracks, structural twisting) that standard auto repair shops are not equipped or qualified to fix.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- General mechanics and panel beaters lack specialist knowledge of caravan body layering, sandwich panels, gas/water plumbing, and timber framing.
- DIY repairs often seal moisture inside, leading to catastrophic internal framing rot and severe loss of vehicle resale value.
- Mobile handymen lack purpose-built spray booths and heavy-duty fabrication equipment needed for structural integrity.
**What it costs them:** Thousands in decayed resale value, denied insurance claims due to improper repairs, canceled holiday trips, and compromised road safety.
**Emotional tension:** Anxiety over hidden water rot, stress after an accident during travel, and frustration with uncertified or sloppy workmanship.

## Competitive Landscape
